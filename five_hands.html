Rules

Divide the 5×5 grid of 25 cards into 5 groups of 5 using the colors.

Each group must be orthogonally connected (no diagonals).

Each group forms a 5-card poker hand. Jokers are wild.

Goal: make your weakest hand as strong as possible.

The puzzle changes daily worldwide.
