Knockout Bot

Challenge the bot in a strategic game of sliding and pushing.

Rules

Normal Move: Slide any distance to an empty square.

Push Move: Move 1 space into a piece. No pushing in the same direction as your previous move.

Goal: Push any one of the Bot's pieces off the board.
