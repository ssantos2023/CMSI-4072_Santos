Rules

Form the grid of 30 cards into 6 five-card poker hands such that your lowest hand is as strong as possible.

First highlight 5 groups of 6 cards using the colors. Each group must be orthogonally connected.

Then select one card from each group (using black ★) to form the sixth hand.

The puzzle changes daily worldwide.
