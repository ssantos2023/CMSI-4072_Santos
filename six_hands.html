Rules

In Six Hands, form the grid of 30 cards into 6 five-card poker hands such that your lowest hand is as strong as possible. Compare with friends to see who can solve for the best low hand.

First, use the 5 non-black colors to highlight 6 groups of cards each. These groups must be fully orthogonally connected.

Then, using the black color, take one card from each group of 6 to form your sixth hand.

Currently, the VALID feature does not check for connectedness, only that you have 5 cards in each hand.

The cards are the same for everyone and change daily.
